What needs to
work better?

New Zealand small businesses have different starting points. Find the situation that sounds familiar, then see the digital work that could help.

People arrive, but they are not sure what you do or what to do next.

Websites & development

Make your offer easier to understand with a website or landing page built around your customers' questions and a clear next step.

Website development · Landing pages

Potential customers are looking, but they are not finding clear answers from you.

SEO & AEO

Organise your pages around what people search for and the questions they ask. SEO focuses on search engines; AEO focuses on how your information is understood by answer tools.

Search engine optimisation · Answer engine optimisation

You have an offer to share and want to reach people beyond your existing audience.

Paid campaigns

Choose advertising channels to fit the audience and the offer, then give people a useful place to land when they click.

  • Google AdsFor people actively searching for what you offer.
  • Meta AdsFor reaching audiences across Facebook and Instagram.
  • TikTok AdsFor a message that works as short-form video.

You want to connect with a specific cultural community in a more considered way.

Ethnic outreach

There is no
one-size-fits-all start.

A small business building its first presence has different needs from one looking to improve what is already live.

Starting from scratch?

Begin with a clear explanation of what you offer and an easy way for people to contact you. A website can give search and advertising somewhere useful to lead.

Already online?

Look at what people can find, what they understand when they arrive and where they stop. That gives you a more useful question to bring to the next conversation.

Tell us where
you are now.

There is no need to have the whole brief worked out. A short email is enough to start the conversation.

A useful first email includes

  • What your business does
  • What is working, or getting in the way, today
  • What you would like to change
